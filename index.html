Welcome to RoutineX

Your premium tool for tracking study routines and completing lectures efficiently.

How to use:

  • Step 1: Go to the Setup tab. Enter your Routine Settings (Phase & Start Date) once.
  • Step 2: Add your subjects by providing the Subject Name, Chapter, and Total Lectures.
  • Step 3: Click "Generate Routine" to view your structured table.
  • Step 4: Download the routine as a high-quality PDF or Image, perfectly formatted for printing on A4 paper.
